Our solutions

Digital menu boards & LED displays

Perfect for: restaurants, cafés, QSR counters, food vendors.

Our solution

We replace printed, laminated menu boards with LED menu displays that restaurants and food vendors can update themselves in minutes — a new special, a sold-out item, a price change — without ever reprinting a board.

We also replace walls of LCD monitors. An LED display lasts considerably longer in a hot, grease-laden kitchen environment, and it is far easier to repair and maintain — a single module is swapped out rather than the whole screen. It also reads as one continuous surface, instead of three or four separate monitors with bezels cutting through the middle of the menu.

Content is split into panels that mirror how a menu is normally read (mains, sides, drinks) and built into the existing counter or ceiling line during fit-out, so the display looks like part of the shop rather than an add-on.
